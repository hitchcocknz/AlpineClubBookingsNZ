## Still open

- **Item 1.** Extending the invoice lookup to Stripe payments risks counting a
  modification refund twice beside the refund credit note #3880 adds for the
  same card refund. The Stripe repair is raise-only, so the worst case today is
  a false divergence alert, never a lost refund. It needs its own trace.
- **Item 4, the stale-link edge.** If a stale allocation link and a newer one
  for the same note and payment are both active, the per-note map keeps
  whichever is read last. No writer is known to leave both active.
- **Item 8.** Waits for #4372 to merge.
- **A writer queued on a keep's locks, in two narrow cases** (`INV-PAY-126`).
  The keep counts a correction already synced in its own transaction. It
  leaves two cases to the recount after its commit: a correction whose sync
  writes its links while the settle runs, and any correction on a payment whose
  refunded total holds more than its rows record, where an older fold may
  already hold it. In those cases a cancel queued on the settle's locks can
  still read the net before that recount. Closing them means deciding the whole
  refunded total inside the settle's transaction. That needs its own issue.
- **Item 11.** Retrying a failed alert needs a sweep for markers whose
  `alertSentAt` is still empty, and #3638's markers share the gap. That is a
  new cron job, outside this issue's bounded scope.
