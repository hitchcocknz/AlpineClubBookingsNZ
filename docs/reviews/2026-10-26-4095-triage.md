## How the fixes are proved

- **Item 2.** `ib-settle-from-cash.realdb.test.ts`, "A CORRECTION ON A LATE
  CAPACITY CANCEL": the whole cash is credited, never as a mixed invoice's part.
- **Item 5 and 12.** `ib-settle-from-cash.realdb.test.ts`, "A CORRECTION SYNCED
  BEFORE A KEPT SETTLE": net $200, not $300, and the note's later sync does not
  count it twice. `kept-settle-correction-recount.test.ts` pins which notes are
  recounted (synced, not voided, reaching a payment) and that a failed recount
  never fails the committed settle.
- **Item 6.** `xero-credit-note-repairs.test.ts` (lost twice; lost once then
  won) and `xero-inbound-reconciliation.test.ts` ("fails a credit-note event
  whose refunded-total claim was lost twice").
- **Item 9.** `xero-inbound-reconciliation.test.ts`, "clears the
  primary-invoice allocation count once Xero lists none".
- **Item 13.** `ib-settled-cash-audit.test.ts`.

The new guards in items 5, 6, 9 and 12 were mutation-checked: each test fails
with its guard removed.

## Still open

- **Item 1.** Extending the invoice lookup to Stripe payments risks counting a
  modification refund twice beside the refund credit note #3880 adds for the
  same card refund. The Stripe repair is raise-only, so the worst case today is
  a false divergence alert, never a lost refund. It needs its own trace.
- **Item 4, the stale-link edge.** If a stale allocation link and a newer one
  for the same note and payment are both active, the per-note map keeps
  whichever is read last. No writer is known to leave both active.
- **Item 8.** Waits for #4372 to merge.
- **Item 11.** Retrying a failed alert needs a sweep for markers whose
  `alertSentAt` is still empty, and #3638's markers share the gap. That is a
  new cron job, outside this issue's bounded scope.
