- **Each lodge now chooses who can be hut leader for school bookings (#3819).**
  The lodge's page under **Admin → Lodges** has a new setting with four kinds
  of leader: a teacher on the booking, the lodge custodian, a member on the
  school booking, and a member staying separately. On a night a school group
  stays, the night counts as covered only when its hut leader is a ticked kind.
  It replaces the club-wide "Assign school teachers as hut leaders" switch in
  Booking Policies: ticking teachers is now what makes approving a school
  booking give its teachers hut-leader PINs, and the upgrade copies the old
  switch into every lodge. Teacher assignments now end on the night before the
  school leaves, and they stop counting if the school booking is cancelled or
  moved.
- **A school booking awaiting its invoice now needs a hut leader.** From the
  moment an approved school booking holds beds, its nights show on the
  dashboard while nobody covers them, and a club member on the school booking
  can be assigned to them, by hand or by the nightly job. The nightly job no
  longer stretches a member's assignment into school nights the lodge does not
  accept them for. A season-long custodian no longer blocks the leader you add
  for school nights when the lodge leaves the custodian unticked. Adding a
  leader whose nights include school nights the lodge does not accept them for
  saves with a note saying so. Teachers become hut leaders at approval only
  while the Hut leaders module is on, and a teacher's assignment can no longer
  be marked as custodian.
