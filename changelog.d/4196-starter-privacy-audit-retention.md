- **Starter privacy policy, section 7 (#4031).** The starter text said system
  audit logs are deleted after 90 days. It now states the real schedule:
  routine diagnostic entries 90 days, sensitive-access records 2 years, change
  and security records 7 years, an optional archive after 12 months up to 7
  years, IP address and browser details removed after 90 days, and incident
  evidence kept for at least 10 years. Only the starter text for new clubs
  changes. **If your club copied the starter privacy policy, review your
  section 7**; saved club pages are not changed.
