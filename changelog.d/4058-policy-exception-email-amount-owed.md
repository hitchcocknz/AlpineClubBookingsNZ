- **The "your request was approved" email now quotes what you actually have to pay (#4058).** When a booking held for a policy exception was approved, the email quoted the booking's full price, even if the member had account credit applied or the booking carried a recorded change fee. It now tells the member the same amount the payment step will ask for (price plus any recorded change fee, less applied credit), and says nothing is owed when credit covers it all.
