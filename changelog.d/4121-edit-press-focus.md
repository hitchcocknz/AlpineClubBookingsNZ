- **Pressing Edit in an admin settings section now moves keyboard focus into
  the section (#4115).** In most settings sections, pressing Edit used to drop
  keyboard and screen-reader users to the top of the page, because the Edit
  button they had just pressed disappeared. Focus now goes to the first field
  that Edit made editable, or to the section itself when there is no such
  field. Add and a row's Edit for booking periods and minimum-stay policies
  now put focus in the form they open. Allocation preferences, club identity
  and member fields keep their current behaviour. Nothing needs configuring.
