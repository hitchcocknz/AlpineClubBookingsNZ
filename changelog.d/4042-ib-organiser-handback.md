- **An organiser's cancel of a group paid by Internet Banking now asks an
  officer to repay the organiser (#4042).** The cancel already recorded each
  joiner's refund on their booking and in Xero, and each joiner's email says
  the club will repay the organiser directly, but nothing asked anyone to send
  the money. The cancel now raises one task in **Money to settle** on the
  payments board, on the organiser's booking, for the total of the joiners'
  refunds with each joiner's share listed, and admins get the **Manual refund
  needed** email once, worded for a group (the cash-booking wording is
  unchanged). Re-running the cancel raises no second task. Marking it paid back
  records only that the money went, because the refunds are already in the
  ledger and Xero. The "Manual refund needed" message gains two tokens,
  `{{handBackNote}}` and `{{handBackLabel}}`, for the paragraph and subject
  phrase that differ between the two cases.
