- **Admins now get an early warning when a credit note is used in Xero while a
  booking still holds that credit (#4220).** The Xero health page has a new "Credit
  used in Xero" card counting the notes affected, and the booking repair check lists
  each booking involved. It only reports: nothing is changed, written to Xero or
  emailed, and it works from what Xero last told the app, so it appears once that
  change has arrived and clears the same way. A booking whose credit Xero has since
  refused is still corrected by the existing automatic fix.
