## Linked Issue

- Closes #4422

## Summary

- Widen the one #3340 superseded-additional-intent selector to include declined (`FAILED`) Stripe asks until their durable cancellation has succeeded.
- Reuse the existing recovery-operation claim and Stripe cancellation processor, including its already-captured hand-off to the superseded refund/treasurer path and its already-cancelled completion path.
- Keep a superseded declined ask's waiting invoice on the later request that carried its balance, including when a stale-tab capture races cancellation after that carrier has paid and released.

## Data Integrity Impact

- A declined superseded ask now owns a durable cancellation row until Stripe confirms cancellation. If capture wins, the existing guarded hand-off records it as a superseded capture and queues its refund or treasurer decision instead of settling or re-invoicing it.
- Captured and refunded ledger rows remain outside the widened selector; an already-successful cancellation also prevents repeat selection.

Focused worker tests and both realdb race orders pin these fences.
